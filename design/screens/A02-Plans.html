Rules the system follows
Dates count both ends. 1 Month from Thu 1 Oct ends Sat 31 Oct.
Buying before a plan ends queues the new one to start the day after.
A pause moves the end date, and every queued plan, back by the paused days.
10 Visits uses one visit per check-in and ends after 6 months.
PT sessions belong to a coach and end with the pack.
Nothing is deleted. Archive hides a member, merge joins two into one.
